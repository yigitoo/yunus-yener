MSE4121 · Res. Asst. Mustafa BORUCU
1What characteristics should a good casting slip have?
It has low viscosity, so it flows easily. The particles do not settle. It has a high solid content (little water). It comes out of the mold easily.
2Describe the interaction between the slip poured into the plaster mold.
The mold is porous. It takes water from the slip. The particles come together and make a solid layer on the mold wall. The layer gets thicker with time.
3What is deflocculant?
It is an additive. It makes the viscosity lower. It makes the particles push each other away, so they do not stick together. Example: sodium silicate.
4How is it determined that the viscosity is at an optimal level?
We add deflocculant step by step and measure the viscosity with a viscometer. The viscosity goes down, then stops going down. This lowest point is the optimum (4–6 Poise). Too much deflocculant makes the viscosity higher again.
