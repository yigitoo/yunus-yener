**[S8] OHS (= OSH)**
- = **multidisciplinary field** → protects **safety, health, and welfare** of people in the workplace
- **Primary goal** → **prevent work-related injuries, illnesses, accidents, and fatalities**
- How? → by **identifying and mitigating** (azaltmak) **environmental hazards**

**[S9] OHS duties**
1. **Identify** potential risks, danger and hazards
2. **Take precautions** against accidents

**[S10] HAZARD** ⭐
- = **any source, situation, or activity with the potential to cause harm**, injury, adverse health effects, or damage to property & environment
- Key phrase → **"the inherent capacity of something to cause harm"**
- **Exists independently of whether an accident has occurred** (kaza olmasa da vardır)

**[S11] 7 Types of Hazards** ⭐
| Type | Keywords (PDF) |
|---|---|
| **Physical** | electricity, heat, **noise**, **radiation**, mechanical energy, machinery without guarding |
| **Chemical** | **toxic**, **corrosive**, **flammable** liquids & gases |
| **Biological** | **bacteria, viruses, fungi, allergens** → healthcare, labs, agriculture |
| **Ergonomic** | **musculoskeletal disorders**, poor workstation design, **repetitive movements**, **heavy lifting** |
| **Psychosocial** | mental & emotional well-being → **stress, bullying, harassment, excessive workload** |
| **Safety** | **slippery floors**, inadequate lighting, **blocked emergency exits** |
| **Environmental** | external → **earthquakes, floods**, pollution, extreme weather |

**[S12] RISK** ⭐
- = **potential for harm** to occur
- 2 components → **Likelihood (probability)** = olma ihtimali · **Severity (consequences)** = olursa ne kadar kötü
- **Risk = Likelihood × Severity**
- Hazard vs Risk → hazard = **source** (yerdeki yağ) · risk = **chance + severity** of harm (kayıp düşme ihtimali × ne kadar kötü)

**[S13] RISK ASSESSMENT** (risk değerlendirmesi) ⭐
- = **systematic process** to **identify, evaluate, and quantify** risks
- Considers **likelihood + severity**
- Purpose → **prioritize hazards** + implement **risk control measures**

**[S14–24] Historical Evolution** ⭐
| When | Keyword |
|---|---|
| **Ancient civilizations** | Egyptians, Greeks, Romans → **rudimentary** (basic) rules for **mining & construction**; Egypt → pharaoh's laws for warriors, embalmers, weapon makers |
| **Hippocrates** | **first** to speak of work-related illness → **lead poisoning** in miners |
| **Medieval Guilds** | groups of craftspeople protecting their craft → safety standards, **apprenticeship training**; surnames from trades |
| **Industrial Revolution** (late 18th–19th c.) | long hours, **child labor**, dangerous machinery → **Factory Acts** (UK), first = **Cotton Mills and Factories Act 1802** → limits on child employment & working hours · **Labor Unions** formed |
| **1911 Triangle Shirtwaist Factory Fire** | New York, **146 deaths** → safety reforms |
| **Workers' Compensation Laws** (early 20th c.) | compensation to injured workers **regardless of fault** |
| **World Wars** | safety rules for **wartime industries** |
| **OSHA 1970** (USA) | federal agency enforcing safety regulations · Turkish equivalent → **İSGGM** (Ministry of Labour and Social Security) |
| **ILO 1919** | international labor standards, workers' rights |
| **ISO 45001 (2018)** | global standard for **OHS management systems** |
| **Contemporary OHS** | technology, **psychosocial & ergonomic** emphasis |

**[S25] Conclusion**
- OHS important for → **human well-being, productivity, legal compliance, cost savings**
- **Shared responsibility** involving **all stakeholders**
