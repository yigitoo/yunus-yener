## 1. History of Aviation — Who = What

### Foreign Pioneers

| Name | Famous for (key hook) |
|------|-----------------------|
| **Leonardo da Vinci** | First scientific drawings of the **helicopter (aerial screw)** & **ornithopters** — studied bird anatomy |
| **Montgolfier Brothers** | **1783** — first **manned hot-air balloon** → start of **lighter-than-air** era |
| **Otto Lilienthal** | **2,000+ glider flights**, curved wing profiles → pioneer of **controllable wing** design |
| **Wright Brothers** (Orville & Wilbur) | **17 Dec 1903**, **Flyer-1** → first **controlled, sustained, powered, heavier-than-air** flight |
| **Louis Blériot** | **1909** — first to fly across the **English Channel**, in his own **monoplane** |

### Turkish Pioneers

| Name | Famous for (key hook) |
|------|-----------------------|
| **İsmail bin Hammad el-Cevheri** | **1002–1010**, wooden wings, jumped from a **mosque roof** → **first Turkish aviation martyr** |
| **Hezarfen Ahmet Çelebi** | Reign of **Murad IV** — glided **Galata Tower → Üsküdar Doğancılar**, crossed the **Bosphorus** |
| **Lagari Hasan Çelebi** | **1633**, **7-barrel gunpowder rocket**, landed in sea with cloth wings → **first manned rocket flight** (+ primitive parachute) |
| **Cavalry Capt. Fesa (Evrensev)** | **Blériot Flying School** (France), **pilot licence no. 1** → **first Turkish military pilot** |
| **Capt. Mehmet Fethi Bey** | **1914 Istanbul–Alexandria** expedition, crashed near **Lake Tiberias** → **first official aviation martyr** (**Fethiye** named after him) |
| **Lt. Nuri Bey** | Same 1914 expedition → **first air mail** mission |
| **Belkıs Şevket Hanım** | **30 Nov 1913**, flew with Fethi Bey → **first Turkish woman in the air** (passenger) |
| **Vecihi Hürkuş** | **First & last flight** of the War of Independence, **first aerial victory**, **Vecihi K-VI** (1st domestic military a/c), **Vecihi XIV** (1st civil a/c), **first private civil airline** |
| **Nuri Demirağ** | **First domestic aircraft factory (Beşiktaş)**, **Nu D.38** passenger a/c, **Gök Okulu (Sky School), Yeşilköy** |
| **Sabiha Gökçen** | **First Turkish female pilot** + **world's first female combat pilot** |
| **Eribe Kartal Hürkuş** | Vecihi's **niece** — **first Turkish female aviation martyr** (parachute failed, **1936** Republic Day show) |

**Karıştırma tuzakları:**
- *First aviation martyr* → **Cevheri** (historical) vs. **Fethi Bey** (first **official**) vs. **Eribe** (first **female**)
- *First woman* → **Belkıs Şevket** (first to fly, passenger) vs. **Sabiha Gökçen** (first **pilot**)
- *1914 expedition* → **Fethi** = martyr, **Nuri** = air mail
- *Firsts in production* → **Vecihi** = aircraft + airline, **Demirağ** = factory + school

## 2. Principles of Flight — 3 Rules

| Rule | Key idea | Keyword |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Bernoulli's Principle** | Speed ↑ → static pressure ↓. Top: fast air = **low P**; bottom: slow air = **high P** → **Lift** | pressure difference |
| **Newton's 3rd Law** | Wing pushes air **down** → air pushes wing **up** (equal & opposite) | **downwash** |
| **Coanda Effect** | Viscous air **clings** to curved upper surface, follows profile, leaves trailing edge downward → links Bernoulli + Newton | streamlines, "sticks to surface" |

## 3. Structural Parts

### Fuselage Types (Airframe / gövde)

| Type | What makes it special | Who carries the load? |
|------|----------------------|------------------------|
| **Truss** (kafes) | Welded steel/Al **tubes in triangles**, **fabric** covered | **Tube skeleton** (tension + compression) |
| **Monocoque** (tek kabuk) | **No internal skeleton**, **eggshell** principle | **Skin only** |
| **Semimonocoque** (yarı monokok) | **Today's aircraft**, light + strong | **Shared:** skin + longitudinal (**longerons, stringers**) + transverse (**formers, bulkheads**) |

### Inside the Wing

| Part | Direction | Job (key hook) |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Spar** (ana kiriş) | **Lengthwise** (fuselage → tip) | **Main load-bearing beam** — carries **all bending & weight** loads |
| **Rib** (kaburga / sinir) | **Transverse** (leading → trailing edge) | Gives the **aerodynamic profile shape**; transfers skin loads **to the spar** |
| **Stringer** (takviye çıtası) | **Lengthwise**, under skin | Prevents skin **waving / buckling**, adds **rigidity** |
| **Skin** (kaplama) | Outer surface | Smooth metal/composite cover; takes **air-pressure loads** → passes to skeleton |

> Hafıza: **Spar = backbone** (eğilme), **Rib = shape** (profil), **Stringer = anti-buckling**, **Skin = cover**.
> Yük yolu: **Skin → Stringer/Rib → Spar → Fuselage**

## 4. Control Surfaces — Which Device Does What?

| Device | Location | Operated by | Motion / Axis |
|--------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Aileron** (kanatçık) | Outer **trailing edge of wing**, move **opposite** | Control column **left–right** | **Roll** — longitudinal axis |
| **Elevator** (irtifa dümeni) | Behind **horizontal stabilizer** | Control column **forward–back** | **Pitch** — lateral axis |
| **Rudder** (istikamet dümeni) | Behind **vertical stabilizer** | **Foot pedals** | **Yaw** — vertical axis |
| **Flap** / **Slat** | Flap = **trailing edge**, Slat = **leading edge** | — | ↑ wing area & camber → **high lift at low speed** (take-off/landing) |
| **Spoiler** (air brake / lift dumper) | **Upper** wing surface, rises up | — | **Destroys lift** → wheels grip runway / air braking |
| **Horizontal & Vertical Stabilizers** | Tail, **fixed (non-moving)** | — | **Stability** (longitudinal + directional) |

> Hafıza: **A-R** (Aileron–Roll), **E-P** (Elevator–Pitch), **R-Y** (Rudder–Yaw).
> **Flap/Slat = lift ↑**, **Spoiler = lift ↓**.

## 5. Engines

### 4 Classic Gas Turbines

| Engine | What makes it special | + | − |
|--------|----------------------|---|---|
| **Turbojet** | **All air** through compressor → combustor → turbine | Pure thrust, high exhaust velocity, **supersonic** | **Very high fuel use & noise** |
| **Turbofan** | Big **front fan**, most air goes through **bypass duct** | **Quietest, most fuel-efficient** → **passenger airliners** | — |
| **Turboprop** | Turbine power → **gearbox → propeller** | **Economical** at low altitude / low speed | **No high speed** (prop tips approach Mach 1) |
| **Turboshaft** | Produces **shaft power**, not thrust | **Helicopter rotors**, **APU** | — |

### Ramjet vs. Scramjet

| | **Ramjet** | **Scramjet** (Supersonic Combustion Ramjet) |
|--|-----------|-----------|
| Moving parts | **None** (no compressor/turbine) | **None** |
| Compression | **Shock waves** at supersonic speed | Shock waves |
| Combustion speed | **Subsonic** | **Supersonic** (no slowing down) |
| Operating range | **Mach 3–6** | **Mach 5+** (hypersonic, **Mach 10+**) |
| Zero speed? | ❌ Needs **rocket booster** | ❌ |
| Main limit | Can't start from standstill | Fuel mixing in **milliseconds** + **friction heating** |

> Tek fark: **Ramjet → subsonic combustion**, **Scramjet → supersonic combustion**.

## 6. Rockets & Electric Propulsion

### 3 Classic Rockets

| Type | What makes it special | + | − |
|------|----------------------|---|---|
| **Solid-fuel** | Fuel + oxidizer as **solid mixture** in the body | **Simple**, **instant high thrust** | **Can't stop or throttle** once lit |
| **Liquid-fuel** | Fuel & oxidizer in **separate tanks**, **pumped** | **Throttleable**, **stop & restart** | Complex pumps/valves → **heavy, costly** |
| **Hybrid** | **Solid fuel + liquid oxidizer** | Solid's **simplicity** + liquid's **shut-off / control** | — |

### Ion / Electric Propulsion

| Item | Key hook |
|------|----------|
| **Principle** | **Ionize xenon** (noble gas) with electricity → accelerate with **electrostatic / electromagnetic fields** |
| **Types** | **Gridded Ion** (electrostatic), **Hall Effect Thruster**, **Pulsed Plasma Thruster** |
| **Strength** | Very high **Isp = 2,000–5,000 s** (fuel efficiency) |
| **Weakness** | **Tiny thrust** ("a coin on your palm") → **cannot lift off from Earth** |
| **Used for** | **Orbit corrections**, **deep-space probes** |

> Kimyasal roket = **high thrust, low Isp** · İyon motor = **low thrust, high Isp**

## 7. Space Hazards — Problem → Effect

| Hazard | On materials / spacecraft | On humans |
|--------|---------------------------|-----------|
| **Thermal Extremes** | Sun vs. shadow: **hundreds of °C** difference → **expansion, thermal stress** | — |
| **Microgravity** | **Fuel/fluid transfer** difficult | **Bone calcium loss**, **muscle weakening** |
| **Space Radiation** (**GCR** + solar particles) | Chip lock-ups / burnouts → **Single Event Effects (SEE)** | **Cancer risk** |
| **High Vacuum** | Needs **airtight pressurized cabin** + **life support** | Body fluids **boil**, tissue damage |
| **Micrometeoroids & Debris** | mm particles at **~10 km/s** pierce **hull & windows** (high KE) | — |
| **Atomic Oxygen** (**LEO**) | Sunlight splits O₂ → reactive **O** → **chemical erosion** of coatings, solar panels, insulation | — |
| **Electrostatic Charging / Arc** | **Space plasma** → voltage builds on **insulating surfaces** → **arcs burn electronics** | — |

> Malzeme dersi için en kritikleri: **Thermal stress**, **Atomic Oxygen erosion**, **Radiation (SEE)**, **Debris impact**.
